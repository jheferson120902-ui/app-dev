## 💰 My Favorite Netflix Series: Money Heist (La Casa de Papel)

**Genre:** Action, Crime, Thriller  
**Created by:** Álex Pina  
**Platform:** Netflix  

---

### 🧠 Summary
*Money Heist* follows a mysterious man known as **The Professor**, who plans the biggest heist in history — printing billions of euros inside the Royal Mint of Spain.  
He recruits eight skilled criminals, each with a city-based code name, to carry out the plan while managing hostages and outsmarting the police.

---

### 🎭 Main Characters
1. **The Professor (Sergio)** – The mastermind behind the heist.  
2. **Tokyo** – The bold and impulsive narrator of the story.  
3. **Berlin** – The calm yet dangerous second-in-command.  
4. **Rio** – The young hacker with a romantic heart.  
5. **Nairobi** – The expert in quality control and heart of the team.  

---

### ❤️ Why I Love It
- Intelligent and unpredictable storyline  
- Deep emotional moments mixed with action  
- Strong teamwork and loyalty among characters  
- Iconic use of *“Bella Ciao”* as a symbol of resistance  

> “The most beautiful things in life are madness.” – *The Professor*
